Readwise Reader

Browse the EPUB books in your Readwise Reader library on the reader and download them to the SD card.

Setup

  • Get your access token at readwise.io/access_token.
  • Open the reader's web page (File Transfer), go to Settings, paste the token on the Readwise Reader card and tap Connect.
  • On the reader, open Home, then Plugins, then Readwise Reader.

What you get

  • Lists for Inbox, Later, Shortlist, Archive and All EPUBs.
  • Books are saved to the Readwise folder on the SD card.

Limits

  • Only EPUBs you uploaded to Reader. Articles, PDFs and other document types are not listed.
  • Each list shows up to 100 books.
  • Books Readwise cannot share as a file, such as Wisereads previews, fail to download.

Privacy

Your token is stored unencrypted in the plugin folder on the SD card and is sent only to readwise.io. Disconnect removes it. Not affiliated with Readwise.
