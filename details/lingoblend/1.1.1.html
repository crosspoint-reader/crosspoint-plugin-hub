LingoBlend

LingoBlend swaps a share of a book's words into the language you are learning, so you pick up new words while you read.

This plugin lists the books you blended at lingoblend.app and downloads them to /LingoBlend. Words you clip while reading go back to your LingoBlend dictionary, matched to the word they replaced, ready to review in the app.

  • Needs a free LingoBlend account. Sign in once: Home > Plugins > LingoBlend > Sign in, then scan the QR code with your phone.
  • To clip a word, press and hold it and choose Clip, or use Reader Menu > Create Clipping.
  • To send clipped words: Home > File Transfer > Join a Network, open the address it shows in a browser, then File Manager > LingoBlend > Sync clippings.
  • For lookups, get the book's dictionary from LingoBlend Dictionaries.
