Almanac: Reading Stats

Reading stats on the official CrossPoint firmware. The reader sends each reading session to Almanac, and your stats show up under Home > Plugins and on the web at almanac.im.

On the reader

  • Today, this week and your day streak.
  • All-time reading time, sessions and books.
  • Every book with its progress, time left and when you'll finish at your pace.
  • A code to open your stats on your phone or computer.

On the web

  • Your reading by day and by hour, and every book with its sessions.
  • More readers and browsers on the same stats.
  • Download your data, or delete it.

Setup

1. Open Home > Plugins > Almanac: Reading Stats and choose Sign in. 2. Scan the QR code with your phone and confirm the code. No account or password.

Notes

  • Nothing to do while reading. The reader sends sessions when it goes to sleep, joining your last Wi-Fi for a few seconds, or when you open the plugin.
  • Only the book's file name and folder, an identifier of the file, reading time and progress leave the reader. Privacy and terms: https://almanac.im/privacy
  • Almanac is in beta: things may change along the way.
