Leaf: Reading Tracker

Read on your reader and keep the record in Leaf without typing anything. How long you read, how many pages and where you stopped arrive on their own.

Leaf is a reading tracker app for iPhone and iPad, coming soon to Android. To use this plugin you need the app on your phone: search the App Store for "Leaf: Reading & Book Tracker". Leaf is free to start, and syncing with the reader needs no subscription.

What you get

  • Every session in the book's reading log, with its time and pages.
  • The book's bookmark moved to where you stopped.
  • Your reading time, speed, pages and streaks in Leaf's stats.
  • At the end of the book, Leaf offers to mark it as read.

How to connect

  • Install the plugin and restart the reader.
  • In Leaf: Settings > E-reader > Connect a reader. Leaf shows a code.
  • On the reader: File Transfer. Open the address it shows in a browser on the same Wi-Fi.
  • In the browser: Settings, the Leaf card. Type the code and press Connect.

The first time you read a book, Leaf asks which of your books it is. After that every session lands on its own.

Good to know

  • Sessions are sent when the reader connects to Wi-Fi or goes to sleep.
  • The reader keeps about a dozen sessions while offline, so connect to Wi-Fi now and then.
  • The plugin sends the book's path on the card, a hash of the file, when and how long you read, and your position in the book. Nothing comes back to the reader.
