Czech E-books (MKP)

Browse and download the free e-books of the Municipal Library of Prague (Městská knihovna v Praze, MKP) on the reader. The catalog and most of the books are in Czech.

The books come from the library's catalog of free e-books: https://www.mlp.cz/katalog/davka/NFW-EKNIHY-VOLNE

Features

  • Over 3,200 free EPUBs: Czech classics, poetry, drama, children's books and a shelf of books in English.
  • Lists of newly added books, classics, poetry, drama, children's books, books in English and everything by author.
  • Search the whole catalog, with or without diacritics: open any list and press Search on its first row.
  • Each title opens a details page with its annotation, year and file size before you download it.
  • Books are saved to /MKP as Author - Title.epub.

Notes

  • Titles the library offers only as PDF are hidden, because the reader can't open them.
  • Books download straight from the library's server. A small proxy only turns the catalog into pages for the reader.
  • Unofficial; not made or endorsed by the Municipal Library of Prague.
