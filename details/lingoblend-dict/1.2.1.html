LingoBlend Dictionaries

Companion dictionaries for LingoBlend's blended books. A blended book has a share of its words swapped into the language you are learning; look one of them up and the reader shows the word it replaced.

  • Free library: dictionaries for LingoBlend's free blended books. No account needed.
  • My dictionaries: the dictionaries for books you blended yourself. Sign in with your LingoBlend account.
  • After downloading, choose it in Settings > Reader > Dictionary. One dictionary is active at a time.
  • Free blended books: add https://www.lingoblend.app/opds/root.xml under Settings > Connectivity > OPDS Servers and pick the underlined edition.
